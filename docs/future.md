## Detection of repeat submissions

Every submission is judged alone in MVP ([Evidence rule](technical/updates.md#evidence-rule)). A repeat of a rejected claim with the same justification gets the same result.

The case for the feature: when 2 submitters observe the same thing and neither justification is strong alone, the 2 observations together are evidence. The first submission left only a `change_requests` row, so nothing links the claim text to a fact, and no search reaches it.

The design:

1. Store an embedding of the `change` text of each request in a `change_requests_vec` table. The worker writes it when the request runs, so `requestChange` stays free of the embedder.
2. In gather, take the top 10 past requests by cosine distance from the new `change` text as a second candidate list.
3. Q1 reads them beside the fact candidates and returns the ones that are the same claim: `{ matches: [...], sameClaim: Array<{ requestId }> }`.
4. Q2 gets the matches with their justification, outcome and reject reason, and this rule:

| Reject reason | Meaning | Second independent submission |
|---|---|---|
| `unsupported` | The justification was too weak to act on. No fact disagreed. | Accepts. Two observations are enough. |
| `contradicted` | A fact disagreed and its justification was judged stronger. | Re-weighs both submissions against the justification of the fact that won. A one-time observation in that fact loses to 2 new observations. A repeatable check wins until a submitter reports that the check now fails. |
